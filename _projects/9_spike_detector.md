A Python toolkit for detecting interictal epileptiform discharges (spikes) in intracranial EEG and quantifying their morphology — rise/fall amplitude and slope, sharpness, line length, and slow-wave features. Detection parameters are optimized for low-frequency stimulation experiments, where spikes have to be reliably separated from stimulation artifact.

This detector underpins much of my published spike-gradient work and the stimulation analysis. Validated against epileptologist review (PPV ≈ 0.90 on the cohort used in the *Epilepsia* paper).
