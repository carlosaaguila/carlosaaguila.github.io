[Camp Kesem](https://campkesem.org) is a national nonprofit that supports children through and beyond a parent's cancer. I have been involved since 2017, first as a counselor and fundraiser at the University of Miami chapter and now as a member of the Graduate Advisory Board.

**Impact to date:**

- $120,000+ raised over my tenure through peer-to-peer fundraising campaigns
- Served as camp counselor providing direct support to children ages 6–18 during week-long summer camp experiences
- Advisory board member contributing to chapter strategy and long-term sustainability

Camp Kesem is the organization I have been involved with longest and the one I hold closest. The work is a consistent reminder of why healthcare research matters beyond the lab.

**Role:** Graduate Advisory Board Member · **Dates:** 2017–Present
