[The Neural Stack](https://neuralmemos.substack.com) (published as Neural Memos on Substack) is a publication I founded in 2025 that translates the technical complexity of the brain-computer interface sector into strategic intelligence for venture capital investors, founders, and operators.

BCI is one of the fastest-moving fields in deep tech, but most coverage either oversimplifies for general audiences or stays too technical for business decision-makers.
The Neural Stack bridges this gap with deep dives into BCI sub-sectors, commercial viability analysis, competitive landscape, and regulatory dynamics.

The publication draws directly on my PhD research in epilepsy neurotechnology and my experience teaching Brain-Computer Interfaces at UPenn since 2023.
