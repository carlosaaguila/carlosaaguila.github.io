[The Neural Stack](https://neuralmemos.substack.com) (published as Neural Memos on Substack) is a publication I founded in 2025 that translates the technical complexity of the brain-computer interface sector into strategic intelligence for venture capital investors, founders, and operators.

BCI is one of the fastest-moving fields in deep tech, but most coverage either oversimplifies for general audiences or stays too technical for business decision-makers. The Neural Stack bridges this gap with:

- Deep dives into BCI sub-sectors (motor BCIs, speech neuroprosthetics, closed-loop neuromodulation, wearables)
- Analysis of commercial viability, key technical hurdles, and competitive landscape
- Coverage of regulatory pathways, reimbursement challenges, and exit dynamics

The publication draws directly on my PhD research in epilepsy neurotechnology and my experience teaching Brain-Computer Interfaces at UPenn.
