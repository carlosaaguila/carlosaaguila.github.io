[Nucleate](https://nucleate.xyz) is a global student-led organization that activates the next generation of biotech founders. I serve as **Co-Head of Technology & Partnership** for the Philadelphia chapter (2025–present).

In this role I:

- Connect academic labs and PhD students with the early-stage startup ecosystem (VCs, law firms, biotech operators)
- Lead technology assessment and partnership development with local life science stakeholders
- Organize programming that helps scientists develop the commercial and entrepreneurial skills needed to translate research into ventures

Philadelphia is one of the most active biotech clusters in the US, with Penn, Drexel, Temple, and a dense network of pharma and medtech companies. Nucleate Philadelphia bridges academic science to that ecosystem.
