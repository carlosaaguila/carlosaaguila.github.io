A deep-learning pipeline for detecting multiple classes of pathological iEEG events — interictal epileptiform discharges (IEDs), high-frequency ripples, and gamma activity — from continuous intracranial recordings. Aimed at replacing brittle threshold-based detectors with a model that generalizes across patients and montages.
